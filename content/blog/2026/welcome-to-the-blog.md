## 1. Hello, world

Welcome! This is my personal corner of the web for sharing what I learn while building software with Dart and Flutter. Expect notes on dependency injection, clean architecture, tooling, and anything else I find worth writing down.

## 2. What you'll find here

A few recurring flavors of posts:

- **Deep dives** — how patterns and tools work under the hood, and why designs end up the way they do.
- **Practical guides** — applying ideas to real Flutter app structures, monorepos, and third-party integrations.
- **Notes & lessons** — small things that save time, discovered the hard way.

All posts live under the [Blog](/blog/) section of this site.

## 3. Get involved

Found a mistake or want to suggest a topic? This site is open source:

- **Report issues** and request topics on [GitHub](https://github.com/chornthorn/tech/issues).
- **Contribute posts** by opening a pull request against the `content/blog` directory.

Happy coding!
